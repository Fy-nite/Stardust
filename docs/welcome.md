welcome to stardust, your personal computing platform designed to allow people over the world to create 
anything and everything that you would want.

Stardust is powered by IKVM, Dotnet and ObjectIR*
(language support subject to change at a later date)
Stardust allow's developers to have a familiar experience to applications like Picotron, tic-80 and libraries like
pi and pyxel by providing a unix like operating system that allows developers to make anything they could ever want.
